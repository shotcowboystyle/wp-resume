Summary

The technical base is strong. Every crawler tested can reach the site, content is server-rendered, every live page self-canonicalizes, titles are unique and sitemap dates reflect real content edits.

One issue carries real risk: product rating markup that is not visible on the page and appears to aggregate third-party reviews. Two sitemap defects and a split organization entity follow. Beyond these, most remaining AI visibility depends on independent reviews and coverage, not on more pages on onpay.com.

Fix first

Ordered by risk, scope and cost to correct.

01 Product rating markup not visible on the page High

Where
/payroll/software/ and 8 industry pages: restaurants, dentists, doctors, farm, gyms, mid-size businesses, nonprofits, startups.
Observed
Each page carries a Product named "Payroll for small businesses" with AggregateRating 4.8 from 762 ratings and 97 reviews, identical on every page. Neither figure appears in the visible text. The page shows G2 and Capterra badges and a "See all ratings and reviews" link instead.
Impact
Google's review snippet guidelines require ratings to be visible on the page and prohibit aggregating ratings from other sites. This pattern risks a structured-data manual action and loss of rich results.
Fix
Remove aggregateRating, or show onsite-collected ratings that match the markup. If the Product node stays, name it as OnPay with a brand; otherwise remove it.

02 Sitemaps advertise broken and empty pages Medium

Where
  • /business-guides/compliance/compliance/ in guide-topic-sitemap: 404
  • /offerings/learn/learn/ in offering-topic-sitemap: 404
  • /schedule/ in page-sitemap (lastmod 2021): 200 with an empty body, no title, H1, canonical or content
Impact
Wastes crawl budget and lowers trust in the sitemap. The doubled slugs suggest a taxonomy sitemap generating term URLs incorrectly. An empty 200 page can be indexed as a soft 404.
Fix
Disable the guide-topic and offering-topic sitemaps in Yoast or correct the term URLs. Remove /schedule/ from the sitemap and either noindex it or return 404 or a redirect.

03 Organization entity split across two nodes Medium

Where
Homepage JSON-LD.
Observed
Yoast outputs #organization with 3 sameAs profiles. A second Organization block has no @id and holds 6 profiles plus legal name, founder, contact point and offer.
Impact
Engines may treat these as two entities, and the YouTube, Instagram and Crunchbase links are attached only to the unidentified one. Consistent entity identity matters for knowledge panels and AI answers.
Fix
Add "@id": "https://onpay.com/#organization" to the second block, or merge its properties into the Yoast graph.

AI search: AEO and GEO

What the site controls, and where the remaining leverage sits.

Owned layer Healthy

Crawlers have access, content is server-rendered, and authorship and dates are explicit. The one open owned fix is item 03, a single organization entity with one complete set of profiles.

/llms.txt returns 404. It is optional and not an evidenced citation lever, so it is not listed as a defect.

Earned layer Main lever

Most of what AI answers say about a payroll brand comes from independent sources: G2, Capterra, editorial reviews, comparison roundups and forums. Review velocity and accurate third-party coverage will move AI visibility more than additional pages on onpay.com.

Method

All 16 child sitemaps under /sitemap_index.xml were parsed (1,194 entries, 1,147 unique URLs). Each URL was fetched without following redirects and its status, canonical, robots directives, title, description, H1 count and JSON-LD were recorded. Rate-limited responses were refetched sequentially. Crawler access was tested by user-agent string, which confirms the response path but not verified crawler IPs or index inclusion.
